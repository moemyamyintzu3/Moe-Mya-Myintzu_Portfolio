💫 About Me

Hi there, I'm Moe Mya Myintzu!👋

I’m a fourth-year software engineering student and UX/UI designer, passionate about the space where code and design meet. That’s where ideas become products people can actually use and enjoy.

Currently, I’m a UX/UI Design Intern at ET.Verdict, where I build and maintain our design system, improve existing experiences, and shape new features. I present my work in weekly team reviews, share ideas, and give feedback to teammates, which has sharpened how I communicate and collaborate.

As a programming course assistant at my university, I helped junior students debug their code and explained not just how to fix errors, but why they happen. That experience taught me to break down complex problems and explain them clearly, a skill I bring to every design and engineering challenge.

I’m calm under pressure and curious about every kind of problem, whether it’s people, design, or code. I care about clean, intuitive design that brings simplicity and joy to users.
Feel free to reach out for a chat, and thanks for stopping by! 💕
