🌙 When I'm Not Working

Outside of work, you’ll find me at my favorite matcha shop with a cup in hand. I’m not a coffee person, matcha is my favorite! 🍵

I’m a naturally curious person. I love exploring new places, travelling, and learning new things. That curiosity follows me into every app and website I use. When I shop on Shopee, I don’t just buy: I look at every feature and wonder why it works the way it does. I noticed the cart had a limit of 99 items, which made saving things for later hard, and then I saw how the “Likes” feature solved that problem. Little details like that are what I love about design.

I also love spending time with my friends. I’m a big listener, and I enjoy hearing what’s on their minds and giving suggestions that help. Understanding people is a big part of who I am, and it’s a big part of why I love UX.

I hope you enjoyed some snippets into my life!♥️
